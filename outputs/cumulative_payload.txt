DRAFT:
**Token Budgeting for AI Agents – A Quick Guide**

1. **Define the token budget** – Establish a hard limit on the total tokens (input + output) an agent may use per interaction, per session, or per day. This ceiling protects costs, ensures consistent latency, and prevents runaway prompts.

2. **Prioritize high‑impact content** – Reserve the majority of the budget for essential instructions, critical context, and the desired answer. Trim or compress peripheral background, optional examples, and decorative language that do not affect the core task.

3. **Use token‑efficient prompting techniques** – Write concise prompts, reuse a single system message across calls, and employ compression tools such as summarizers or embeddings. Favor structured formats (JSON, tables, bullet lists) that convey the same information with fewer tokens than free‑form text.

4. **Monitor and enforce usage in real time** – Log token counts per request, set alerts for approaching limits, and enforce hard caps that automatically truncate or reject prompts that would exceed the budget.

5. **Iteratively optimize and adapt** – Regularly review usage patterns, pinpoint spikes, and refine budgets, templates, or model choices. Continuous tuning keeps the agent performant while staying within token constraints.
